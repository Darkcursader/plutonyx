01  /  Dawn

Share out
the power.

Each sol opens with a morning report. Then you split your cells between the Air Maker, the Greenhouse, the Storm Shield and the Rover. Only three meters ever sit on screen: Air, Power, Shield. Food, water and the radio arrive as events.

02  /  Consequence

Nothing
is free.

There is no safe option. What you choose today lands sols later, and the game tells you why — a quiet line that begins with “because you…”

03  /  The sky

The weather
is real.

Every sol is one real day of the Sun’s recorded history. The dose on your counter is what a rover actually measured on the surface of Mars that day. Today’s real sky can even build a mission from the last thirty days.

04  /  Sol 8

You decide
alone.

A storm arrives that no forecast listed. Earth can’t see that side of the Sun. A question and its answer need 32 minutes; the radiation peaks in 39. At the end, the game tells you: it really happened.

05  /  Outside

Step out
onto the crater.

Walk the outpost between decisions — the habitat, the greenhouse, the solar field, the pad where the ascent ship waits — on terrain built from real elevation data of Jezero Crater, at true vertical scale.
